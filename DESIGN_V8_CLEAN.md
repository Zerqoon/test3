# R3V0 Player History V8 — Clean Asset Edition

This revision keeps the generated fantasy asset pack but reduces visual noise.

Changes:
- clean data panels: no full ornamental frame stacked over each panel,
- darker/cleaner background veil so text and values stay readable,
- central clan logo remains the focal point,
- pillars, pets, crystals and platform are used only as restrained hero accents,
- contribution history remains a glowing purple bar chart,
- chart decorations are limited to small corner crystal accents,
- player avatar remains circular in the PLAYER card.

All generated assets remain included under `assets/branding/` and `assets/branding/ui/`.
